Project overview & educational purpose

Accessible data about domestic energy.

This website was developed as an educational demonstration project for COS30045 Data Visualisation at Swinburne University of Technology, Melbourne, Australia.

Its purpose is to provide Australian consumers and students with accessible, data-informed insights into how domestic appliances consume electrical energy and affect household utility budgets through clear visual representation and intuitive exploratory design.
